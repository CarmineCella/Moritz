# Library: abstractions in library/, made of other objects (double-click one to open it)

@ reverb~
syntax: reverb~ [mix] [T60]
A stereo Freeverb. Inlets: the signal, the wet/dry mix (0 dry to 1 wet, a
20 ms ramp) and the decay time T60 in seconds (0.1 to 100). Outlets: left
and right. Per channel, eight damped combs (fvcomb~) in parallel, then four
allpass~ (gain 0.5) in series; the right channel's delays are 23 samples
longer (at 44.1 kHz), so the two outputs are decorrelated. Each comb's
feedback is g = 10^(-3 d / T60) for its delay d in seconds, so the tail falls
by 60 dB in T60 seconds, the highs a little faster (damping).
arg mix: the wet/dry mix, 0 to 1 (0 or none: 0.3)
arg T60: the decay time, in seconds, 0.1 to 100 (0 or none: 2)
see: fvcomb~ allpass~ comb~ tapin~ conv~

@ fvcomb~
syntax: fvcomb~ ms name
One Freeverb comb filter, used by reverb~: a feedback comb with a one-pole
low-pass in the loop (damping), as in Jezar's Freeverb. One signal inlet, one
signal outlet. Its decay time T60 (seconds) arrives on the receive name
`name-t60`; the feedback g = 10^(-3 d / T60) for its delay d, ramped over
50 ms, so every comb dies away in the same time.
arg ms: the delay, in milliseconds (100 at most)
arg name: the prefix of the receive that sets the decay: name-t60
see: reverb~ comb~ tapin~ tapout~ biquad~

@ impose~
syntax: impose~ [order]
The spectral envelope of one sound imposed on another (Musil's
impose-envelope): the carrier speaking with the modulator's formants, an
orchestra or a synth that speaks with a voice. Inlets: the modulator (whose
colour is taken), the carrier (what sounds), the envelope order. One signal
outlet. Built from specenv~ in a pfft~ of 1024 points, overlap 4
(impose-fx): the carrier's fine structure times the modulator's envelope.
arg order: the envelope order, its resolution: about sr / (2 f0) of the modulator (0 or none: 60)
see: specenv~ pfft~ sparkle~ pitchshift~ impose-fx

@ impose-fx
syntax: impose-fx
The spectral patch of impose~, run by its pfft~ once a frame: the carrier's
amplitudes divided by their envelope (its fine structure) times the
modulator's envelope, with the carrier's phases. fftin~ 1 is the modulator,
fftin~ 2 the carrier, and the control inlet the envelope order of both
specenv~. Internal: use impose~.
see: impose~ pfft~ specenv~ cartopol~

@ binpan~
syntax: binpan~ [azimuth] [elevation]
A mono sound placed around the listener, for headphones. Inlets: the signal,
the azimuth in degrees (0 in front, 90 left, -90 right, 180 behind), the
elevation in degrees (up). Outlets: left and right. An order-2 ambisonic
encoder (ambienc~ 2) into binaural~ 2: each binpan~ has its own binaural~, so
for many sources sum their ambisonic fields and decode them with a single
binaural~.
arg azimuth: the starting azimuth, in degrees (0)
arg elevation: the starting elevation, in degrees (0)
see: ambienc~ binaural~ hrtf~ ambirot~

@ pitchshift~
syntax: pitchshift~ [semitones]
A live pitch shifter. Inlets: the signal, the transposition in semitones
(decimals allowed), formant preservation 1/0 (on at the start: a voice keeps
its timbre; 0: the formants move with the pitch). One signal outlet, a window
(2048 samples) late. It is sparkle~ without a buffer~, its pitch ratio
2^(semitones / 12), with envelope 60 for the formants.
arg semitones: the starting transposition, in semitones (0)
see: sparkle~ impose~ specenv~ pfft~
