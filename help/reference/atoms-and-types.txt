# Atoms and types: matching pursuit (after Maple) and sound types (after SoundTypes)

@ atoms
syntax: atoms name
A dictionary for pursuit, shared by name: pursuit and soundtypes find it by
its name. It holds parametric atoms (gabor, gammatone:
windowed cosines at every scale, position and frequency) or atoms of sound
cut from recordings, each of unit energy. It starts empty; building one from
files runs as a background job, and the number of atoms comes out when it is
ready (for gabor and gammatone, the number of scales).

The folder messages read every WAV, AIFF, FLAC and MP3 file of a folder (not
its subfolders), mixed to mono at 44.1 kHz, and replace the dictionary; `add`
appends to a dictionary of sounds. Right-click the box for Info and Save the
dictionary...
arg name: the name pursuit, soundtypes and the others use (required)
msg gabor [min max] [fmax]: Gaussian-windowed cosines, window lengths from min to max samples (powers of 2, from 32 to 65536; 256 and 8192), frequencies from 40 Hz up to fmax (16000), at the audio's sample rate
msg gammatone [min max] [fmax]: the same with a gammatone envelope (sharp attack, slow decay): good for attacks
msg frames folder [ms] [hop]: every sound file of a folder cut into Hann-windowed frames of ms milliseconds (100), one every hop ms (the same as ms)
msg onsets folder [threshold] [max]: the files cut at their onsets (threshold 2), each piece max ms long at most (1000); pieces under 20 ms are skipped
msg files folder [max]: every file whole, max ms at most (2000)
msg add buffer [frames ms hop | onsets threshold max | whole]: adds atoms cut from a buffer~ (whole by default, 2000 ms at most), at the dictionary's sample rate
msg get k buffer: writes atom k (from 0) of a dictionary of sounds into a buffer~, to listen to it
msg clear: empties the dictionary
msg write file: saves the dictionary (a .atoms file)
msg read file: loads a dictionary saved with write
msg info: prints its kind and size in the Console
msg stop: cancels the job
see: pursuit soundtypes buffer~ onsets

@ pursuit
syntax: pursuit
Matching pursuit, as a background job: a buffer~ explained as a sum of atoms
of a dictionary (an atoms object), the strongest first, then rebuilt,
transformed or hybridised. With gabor and gammatone atoms it is an STFT per
scale with local updates (every position on a grid of a quarter of the scale,
the frequency refined by a parabola, the phase by an exact projection); it
stops after `atoms` atoms or when the residual is `snr` dB under the sound.
With atoms of sound the target is cut into segments (at its onsets, or in
blocks), and each is explained by `components` atoms, each slid to its best
place by FFT cross-correlation among the `candidates` closest in mel
spectrum.

The decomposition (the book) is a buffer~ of 8 channels, one frame an atom,
at the sound's sample rate: time (s, the atom's centre), frequency (Hz),
level (dB), dictionary atom (-1 for parametric atoms), length (samples),
amplitude, phase, start (samples). plot in scatter mode draws it as an atom
map. After a run the number of atoms and the signal-to-residual ratio (dB)
come out. A result that would clip is scaled down, with a message in the
Console.
msg run sound dictionary book [residual]: decomposes the buffer~ sound (mixed to mono) with the atoms dictionary into the buffer~ book; what is left goes to the buffer~ residual if named
msg synth book dictionary out: rebuilds a sound from a book into the buffer~ out, with ratio, stretch, tails and keep
msg cross book from to out: rebuilds a book made with dictionary from using the closest atoms (in mel spectrum) of dictionary to: both must be dictionaries of sounds
msg atoms n: parametric atoms: the most atoms to find (2000)
msg snr dB: parametric atoms: stops when the residual is this far under the sound (30)
msg components n: sound atoms: atoms a segment, 1 to 64 (4)
msg candidates n: sound atoms: the atoms tried by cross-correlation in each segment (16)
msg segments onsets | blocks: sound atoms: cut the target at its onsets (default) or into blocks
msg block ms: the length of the blocks, and the longest segment (100)
msg threshold x: the onset threshold for segments onsets (2)
msg ratio r: synthesis: transposition, 0.05 to 20 (1)
msg stretch s: synthesis: time stretch, 0.05 to 50 (1)
msg tails 1/0: synthesis: 1, sound atoms ring whole; 0, they stop where they matched (1)
msg keep f: synthesis: the share of the atoms used, strongest first, 0 to 1 (1)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: atoms soundtypes typematch plot buffer~ nmf

@ soundtypes
syntax: soundtypes name
A sound analysed into types, after SoundTypes (ICMC 2013), as a background
job, and shared by name with typegen and typematch. Level 1: the frames of
the sound (13 MFCCs, centroid and level; with `pitch 1` also the 12 pitch
classes) or the atoms of a pursuit book are clustered into `types` types
(k-means++) and smoothed; runs of one type are the units. With `segment
onsets` the units are instead the notes or strokes between onsets, described
and clustered as wholes. Levels 2 and 3 group the units of the level below
into gestures, cut where the next type is surprising, then cluster them.

After an analysis the number of units of each level comes out as a list.
Right-click the box for Info and Write the units as text...
arg name: the name typegen and typematch use (required)
msg analyse sound: analyses a buffer~ (mixed to mono)
msg analyse book dictionary: analyses the atoms of a pursuit book instead (a book is always cut into frames); its units are rebuilt from their atoms
msg segment frames: level 1 from runs of frames of one type (default)
msg segment onsets [threshold] [gap]: level 1 from the spans between onsets (threshold 1.5; onsets at least gap ms apart, 80)
msg pitch 1/0: adds the pitch classes (chroma) to the frame features, so that types follow harmony too (0)
msg types k1 [k2 k3]: the number of types at each level, 2 to 64 (12 6 4)
msg levels n: the number of levels, 1 to 3 (2)
msg threshold z: how surprising a type must be to end a gesture: its surprise above the mean plus z standard deviations (1)
msg smooth n: level-1 labels take the majority over n frames, made odd (5)
msg size n: the analysis window, a power of 2 from 256 to 16384 samples (2048)
msg hop n: the step between frames, 32 to 8192 samples (512)
msg seed n: the seed of the clustering (1)
msg labels buffer: writes the type of every frame into a buffer~, one channel a level: plot it with colors types
msg transitions level buffer: writes the probabilities of passing from type to type at a level into a buffer~ (a matrix)
msg play level type: outputs the start and end (ms) of a unit of that type, chosen at random
msg write file: writes the units as text, one a line: level start end type (seconds)
msg info: prints the units and types of each level in the Console
msg stop: cancels the job
see: typegen typematch pursuit atoms plot kmeans

@ typegen
syntax: typegen [model]
New sequences of the types of a soundtypes model, at one of its levels: the
classic n-grams of SoundTypes, a variable-order Markov model, or a factor
oracle that follows the original and jumps where the recent past repeats
(the default: continuity 0.8, context 1). `generate` realises the units with
their own sound (or their atoms), joined by short crossfades, as a background
job; `next` walks an endless sequence live, one unit at a time, to drive
play~ or groove~ on the original.
arg model: the name of a soundtypes object
msg generate seconds out [labels]: writes a new sound of about this length into the buffer~ out, and its types into the buffer~ labels if named (for plot)
msg next: the next unit of an endless sequence: its start and end (ms) in the original, then its type
msg reset: starts the live sequence again
msg classic n: SoundTypes' n-grams: a context of n-1 types (2); a dead end starts again elsewhere
msg markov [order] [count]: variable order: the longest context, up to order units (3), seen at least count times (2)
msg oracle [continuity] [context]: factor oracle: follows the original with probability continuity, 0 to 1 (0.8), otherwise jumps to where the last context units (1) repeat
msg level n: generates units of level n, 1 to 3 (1)
msg inner c [context]: levels mixed: units of level 2 or 3 are played with the level below regenerated, an oracle walk of continuity c (0 to 1) and context (1) from each one's first sub-unit; 1 (default) plays them as they are
msg model name: uses another soundtypes object
msg seed n: the seed of the random choices (1)
msg xfade ms: the crossfade between units, 0.5 to 500 ms (10)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: soundtypes typematch play~ groove~ plot

@ typematch
syntax: typematch
SoundTypes' rematch, as a background job: every frame, unit or gesture of
sound A replaced by the closest one of sound B (duration included) in its
closest type. A and B are soundtypes models analysed the same way (both
sounds, or both books). At level 0, B's frames are taken as Hann grains of
its analysis size and overlap-added at A's frames (the original rematch); at
level 1 unit by unit; at level 2 gesture by gesture. A result that would clip
is scaled down.
msg run A B out [level] [labels]: rebuilds A with the material of B into the buffer~ out; the types of B over A's time go to the buffer~ labels if named
msg level n: 0, frame by frame; 1, unit by unit; 2 or 3, by the units of that level (gestures) (1)
msg space own | joint: features standardised in each sound's own space, so that B's range covers A's (default), or in one space for both (absolute distances)
msg fit 1/0: 1, at A's times and durations (B's units cut or continued); 0, one after the other at B's own durations (1); not used at level 0
msg contour c: A's loudness imposed on the result by c, 0 (B's own) to 1 (A's), so that A's rhythm and phrasing come through (0; the message alone sets 1); above level 0, only with fit 1
msg xfade ms: the crossfade between units, 0.5 to 500 ms (10)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: soundtypes typegen pursuit atoms
