# Spatial: ambisonics (AmbiX: ACN order, SN3D) and binaural sound
#
# Azimuth in degrees, 0 in front, positive to the left; elevation in degrees,
# positive up. A sound field of order N has (N+1)^2 channels: W Y Z X at order 1.

@ ambienc~
syntax: ambienc~ [order] [azimuth] [elevation]
Encodes a mono signal at a direction as an ambisonic sound field of
(order+1)^2 channels (AmbiX: ACN order, SN3D; at order 1, W Y Z X). Azimuth
and elevation are numbers or signals; moving sources glide, the gains are
interpolated over each block. Fields add channel by channel, so several
sources can be summed before decoding.
arg order: the ambisonic order, 1 to 5 (1)
arg azimuth: degrees, 0 in front, 90 to the left (0)
arg elevation: degrees, positive up (0)
see: ambirot~ ambidec~ binaural~ hrtf~ binpan~

@ ambirot~
syntax: ambirot~ [order] [yaw]
Turns a sound field about the vertical axis by the yaw in its last inlet,
exactly: within each order the pair of channels m, -m rotates by m times the
yaw. To follow a listener's head, rotate by minus the head's yaw.
arg order: the ambisonic order, 1 to 5 (1)
arg yaw: the rotation in degrees, positive to the left (0)
msg number: in the last inlet, the yaw (degrees)
see: ambienc~ ambidec~ binaural~

@ ambidec~
syntax: ambidec~ order azimuth ...
Decodes a sound field to loudspeakers on a horizontal ring, one outlet per
azimuth, in the order given (ambidec~ 1 45 135 -135 -45 is a square: front
left, back left, back right, front right). It uses the horizontal channels
only: for an evenly spaced ring of at least 2N+1 speakers this is the exact
(mode-matching) decoder, with 2D max-rE weights.
arg order: the ambisonic order, 1 to 5 (1)
arg azimuth: the speakers' azimuths in degrees, one per outlet (45 135 -135 -45 when none is given)
see: ambienc~ ambirot~ binaural~ dac~

@ binaural~
syntax: binaural~ [order]
Plays a sound field on headphones. The field is decoded to 17 virtual
speakers over the sphere (8 at ear height, 4 above, 4 below, 1 overhead),
each heard through the measured head-related impulse responses of its
direction (MIT KEMAR), with SN3D normalisation and 3D max-rE weights. All of
it folds into one filter pair per channel, so the cost does not depend on
the number of sources. A source in front plays at the level of the front
head response.
arg order: the ambisonic order, 1 to 5 (1)
see: ambienc~ ambirot~ hrtf~ binpan~

@ hrtf~
syntax: hrtf~ [azimuth] [elevation]
Places a mono sound at a direction on headphones through the measured
head-related impulse responses themselves (all 710 directions of the MIT
KEMAR set): the four measured directions around the one asked for,
interpolated. Without ambisonics in between, the cues of the outer ear stay
sharp, so height is clearer than with binaural~, and the level follows the
real head (quieter behind and above). Azimuth and elevation are numbers or
signals; a moving direction crossfades within each block.
arg azimuth: degrees, 0 in front, 90 to the left, 180 behind (0)
arg elevation: degrees, -40 to 90 (0)
see: binaural~ ambienc~ binpan~
