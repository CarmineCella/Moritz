# Analysis: probes, meters, live descriptors, and the offline analyses of a buffer~

@ snapshot~
syntax: snapshot~ [ms]
Reports the current value of its signal (the last sample of the latest audio
block) as a number: on bang, and every `ms` milliseconds while audio runs. An
interval of 0 reports only on bang; the shortest interval is 5 ms.
arg ms: the report interval, in milliseconds (0: only on bang)
msg bang: reports the value now
msg number: sets the report interval (ms); also the right inlet
msg interval ms: sets the report interval (ms)
see: peakamp~ meter~ scope~ descriptors~ sig~

@ peakamp~
syntax: peakamp~ [ms]
Reports the largest absolute value its signal reached since the last report,
then starts measuring again: on bang, and every `ms` milliseconds while audio
runs (5 ms at least). Use atodb to read it in dB.
arg ms: the report interval, in milliseconds (0: only on bang)
msg bang: reports the peak now
msg number: sets the report interval (ms); also the right inlet
msg interval ms: sets the report interval (ms)
see: snapshot~ meter~ atodb descriptors~

@ meter~
syntax: meter~
A level meter: the peak of its signal drawn as a vertical bar from -60 dB to
0 dB, green, yellow above -12 dB, red above -3 dB. The bar falls back
gradually, so short peaks stay visible for a moment.
see: peakamp~ scope~ spectroscope~ gain~

@ scope~
syntax: scope~ [samples]
An oscilloscope: the latest `samples` of its signal, from -1 to 1, triggered
on a rising zero crossing so that a periodic wave stands still.
arg samples: how many samples the display spans, 32 to 1024 (512)
see: meter~ spectroscope~ waveform~ snapshot~ plot

@ spectroscope~
syntax: spectroscope~ [width height]
The live spectrum of its signal: a 2048-point FFT with a Hann window,
frequency on a log scale from 20 Hz to half the sample rate, level from 0 dB
(a full-scale sine) down to -90 dB, with grid lines at 100 Hz, 1 kHz and
10 kHz and at -30 and -60 dB. Peaks fall back slowly, so short sounds stay
visible for a moment.
arg width: the width of the display, in pixels, 120 to 2000 (300)
arg height: the height of the display, in pixels, 60 to 1000 (130)
see: scope~ meter~ spectrogram descriptors~ melbands~

@ descriptors~
syntax: descriptors~ [size] [hop] [names...]
Low-level descriptors of its signal, measured over the last `size` samples
every `hop` samples while audio runs, one outlet each. The names choose the
outlets and their order; without names there are the eleven numbers, in this
order: rms (root mean square level), centroid (Hz, the amplitude-weighted mean
frequency), spread (Hz, the standard deviation around the centroid),
skewness, kurtosis (3 for a Gaussian shape), flux (how much the spectrum grew
since the last frame), hfc (high frequency content: the energy weighted by
bin number), zcr (zero crossings per second), flatness (0 tonal to 1 noisy),
periodicity (0 to 1) and f0 (Hz). Three names give lists instead: mfcc (13
MFCCs over 40 mel bands, as mfcc~), chroma (the 12 pitch classes, as chroma~)
and melbands (40 mel bands in dB, as melbands~), at this box's size. All of
them come from one FFT a frame.

f0 is found by YIN (de Cheveigné and Kawahara): the first period at which the
frame's normalised difference with itself dips under the threshold (0.1),
refined between samples and checked against the windows around it. A weak or
missing fundamental does not send it an octave up. It is 0 when no period
dips under the threshold (noise, silence), and is searched between 50 and
4000 Hz (range), and no lower than 2 sr / size (43 Hz with size 2048 at
44.1 kHz): a larger size reaches lower notes. periodicity is 1 minus the
normalised difference at that period: 1 for a periodic sound, near 0 for
noise. The formulas are Musil's (descriptors, yin, spectral-moments...), so a
script gets the same numbers.
arg size: the frame, in samples, a power of 2 from 256 to 32768 (2048)
arg hop: the step between analyses, in samples, 16 or more (1024)
arg names: any of rms centroid spread skewness kurtosis flux hfc zcr flatness periodicity f0 mfcc chroma melbands (the eleven numbers)
msg bang: analyses the latest frame now
msg hop n: sets the step between analyses (samples)
msg threshold t: sets YIN's threshold, 0.001 to 1 (0.1); higher calls more frames voiced
msg range lo hi: sets the f0 searched, in Hz (50 4000)
see: melbands~ mfcc~ chroma~ onset~ knn plot

@ spectrogram
syntax: spectrogram [size] [hop]
Writes the magnitude spectrogram of a buffer~ into another buffer~, as a
background job: one channel a bin (size / 2 + 1 of them, or up to `maxfreq`),
one sample a frame, values in dB (0 dB: a full-scale sine; down to -100). The
result's sample rate is the number of frames a second, so plot shows its time
axis. A Hann window of `size` samples, a frame every `hop`; a stereo sound is
mixed to mono.
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 65536 (2048)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (512)
msg run sound matrix: analyses the buffer~ sound and writes the spectrogram into the buffer~ matrix
msg maxfreq Hz: keeps only the bins up to this frequency (0: all of them)
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: melgram chromagram plot similarity spectroscope~ buffer~

@ melgram
syntax: melgram [bands] [size] [hop]
Writes the mel spectrogram of a buffer~ into another buffer~, as a background
job: the energy (dB) in `bands` triangular filters spaced evenly on the mel
scale, the ear's resolution. One channel a band (lowest first), one sample a
frame, at the number of frames a second.
arg bands: the number of mel bands, 2 to 256 (40)
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 65536 (2048)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (512)
msg run sound matrix: analyses the buffer~ sound and writes the mel spectrogram into the buffer~ matrix
msg bands n: sets the number of bands
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: mfccgram spectrogram melbands~ similarity plot

@ mfccgram
syntax: mfccgram [coeffs] [bands] [size] [hop]
Writes the mel-frequency cepstral coefficients of a buffer~ into another
buffer~, as a background job: the cosine transform of each mel frame, a
smooth description of the spectral envelope (the timbre) in a few numbers;
coefficient 0 is the overall level. One channel a coefficient, one sample a
frame. There are never more coefficients than bands.
arg coeffs: the number of coefficients, 1 to 128 (13)
arg bands: the number of mel bands they are computed from, 2 to 256 (40)
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 65536 (2048)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (512)
msg run sound matrix: analyses the buffer~ sound and writes the MFCCs into the buffer~ matrix
msg coeffs n: sets the number of coefficients
msg bands n: sets the number of mel bands
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: melgram mfcc~ similarity soundtypes plot

@ chromagram
syntax: chromagram [size] [hop]
Writes the chromagram of a buffer~ into another buffer~, as a background job:
12 channels, C C# D ... B, how much of each pitch class sounds, every frame
scaled to a maximum of 1 (silence stays 0). The pitch classes are read from
the spectral peaks (as HPCP), each given to its nearest note from C2 to B6;
a peak that is probably the 3rd, 5th, 6th or 7th partial of a lower one
counts a tenth. A large window resolves the low notes.
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 256 to 65536 (4096)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (1024)
msg run sound matrix: analyses the buffer~ sound and writes the chromagram into the buffer~ matrix
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: chroma~ keyfind similarity plot spectrogram

@ onsets
syntax: onsets [threshold] [size] [hop]
Finds where the notes and hits of a buffer~ begin, as a background job: the
peaks of the spectral flux (how much the spectrum grows from frame to frame)
above `threshold` times its moving median, at least `gap` ms apart, each
placed at the sample where the energy jumps. The times (ms) come out as a
list.

The onsets cut the sound into segments, the first from the beginning and the
last to the end: after a run, the number of segments comes out, and `segment
k` outputs the start and end of one of them, ready for play~.
arg threshold: how far above the median a peak must be, as a factor (2)
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 65536 (1024)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (256)
msg run sound [curve]: finds the onsets of the buffer~ sound; the flux goes to the buffer~ curve if one is named
msg segment k: outputs the start and end (ms) of segment k, counting from 0 (k wraps around)
msg threshold x: sets the threshold factor (2)
msg width n: the frames of the moving median, made odd (21)
msg gap ms: the shortest time between two onsets (50)
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: onset~ beats novelty play~ atoms soundtypes

@ beats
syntax: beats [size] [hop]
Estimates the tempo of a buffer~ and tracks its beats, as a background job
(Ellis 2007): the onset strength (spectral flux), its autocorrelation read
from 30 to 300 bpm and weighted by a prior on the tempo listeners tap, then
dynamic programming for beats that are both strong and regular. The tempo
(bpm) and the beat times (ms, a list) come out.
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 65536 (1024)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (256)
msg run sound [curve]: finds the tempo and the beats of the buffer~ sound; the onset strength goes to the buffer~ curve if one is named
msg tempogram sound matrix: writes the tempogram: 4 frames a second, one channel a bpm from 30 (channel 0) to 300, how periodic the onsets are at that tempo (1: perfectly)
msg clicks buffer: after a run, writes a click track into a buffer~: as long as the sound, a short 1 kHz click on every beat
msg prior bpm octaves: the centre of the tempo prior, 30 to 300 bpm (100), and its width in octaves (0.6)
msg tightness x: how strongly the beats keep to the tempo (100)
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: onsets onset~ keyfind metro plot

@ keyfind
syntax: keyfind [size] [hop]
Estimates the key of a buffer~, as a background job: the chromagram averaged
over the sound (a pitch-class distribution) correlated with the
Krumhansl-Kessler profile of every major and minor key (Krumhansl 1990). The
key ("A minor"), its correlation (-1 to 1), the distribution (12 values, C
first, a maximum of 1) and the correlations with all 24 keys (C major, C
minor, C# major ... B minor) come out.
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 256 to 65536 (4096)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (1024)
msg run sound [from to]: the key of the buffer~ sound, or of the part between from and to (ms)
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: chromagram chroma~ beats

@ similarity
syntax: similarity [frames]
Writes the self-similarity matrix of a feature matrix (a chromagram, an MFCC
matrix...) into a buffer~, as a background job: the cosine similarity of
every frame with every other, 1 where two moments sound alike (Foote 1999).
The structure of a piece becomes a picture: sections are squares on the
diagonal, repeats are stripes beside it. Frames are averaged in groups so
that the matrix has at most `frames` of them.
arg frames: the most frames the matrix has, 16 to 4000 (1000)
msg run features matrix: writes the self-similarity of the buffer~ features into the buffer~ matrix
msg frames n: sets the most frames (1000)
msg standardize 0/1: 1 takes each feature's mean off and scales it to unit variance first, so that MFCC coefficient 0 does not dominate (0)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: novelty chromagram mfccgram plot

@ novelty
syntax: novelty [width] [threshold]
Finds the section boundaries of a piece from its self-similarity matrix, as a
background job (Foote 2000): a checkerboard kernel of `width` frames slides
along the diagonal, high where what comes before is unlike what comes after.
The curve goes to a buffer~; the boundaries (ms) are its peaks above
`threshold` times its moving median, at least `gap` ms apart, and come out as
a list. They cut the piece into sections: after a run the number of sections
comes out, and `section k` outputs the start and end of one of them.
arg width: the width of the kernel, in frames of the matrix, 2 to 512 (32)
arg threshold: how far above the median a peak must be, as a factor (1.5)
msg run similarity curve: reads the square matrix in the buffer~ similarity and writes the novelty curve into the buffer~ curve
msg section k: outputs the start and end (ms) of section k, counting from 0 (k wraps around)
msg width n: sets the kernel width (frames)
msg threshold x: sets the threshold factor
msg gap ms: the shortest time between two boundaries (2000)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: similarity onsets plot soundtypes

@ curves
syntax: curves [size] [hop]
Curves of a buffer~ over time, written to another buffer~ as a background
job, one value every `hop` samples. Frame by frame the curves are jumpy:
`smooth` passes them through a moving median.
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 65536 (2048)
arg hop: the step between frames, in samples, 16 or more (512)
msg loudness sound curve: the level in dB, roughly as the ear weighs it (below 150 Hz and above 8 kHz taken out; 0 dB a full-scale sine)
msg register sound curves: 3 channels: the spectral centroid in MIDI pitch, the spread around it in octaves, the lowest strong partial in MIDI pitch
msg polyphony sound curve: how many voices seem to sound, from the strong spectral peaks, about three a voice (a rough estimate, 0 in silence)
msg smooth n: the frames of the moving median, made odd (1: no smoothing)
msg size n: sets the window (samples)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
msg stop: cancels the job
see: descriptors~ spectrogram plot soundtypes

@ melbands~
syntax: melbands~ [bands] [size] [hop]
The live mel spectrum of its signal: a list of `bands` values in dB, measured
over the last `size` samples every `hop` samples while audio runs. The live
version of melgram; into a plot in image mode, the lists scroll by as a
picture.
arg bands: the number of mel bands, 2 to 256 (40)
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 32768 (2048)
arg hop: the step between analyses, in samples, 16 or more (512)
msg bang: analyses the latest window now
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
see: mfcc~ chroma~ melgram descriptors~ plot

@ mfcc~
syntax: mfcc~ [coeffs] [bands] [size] [hop]
The live MFCCs of its signal: a list of `coeffs` cepstral coefficients of its
mel spectrum, every `hop` samples while audio runs; coefficient 0 is the
level. The live version of mfccgram, and a compact description of timbre for
knn or kmeans.
arg coeffs: the number of coefficients, 1 to the number of bands (13)
arg bands: the number of mel bands, 2 to 256 (40)
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 32768 (2048)
arg hop: the step between analyses, in samples, 16 or more (512)
msg bang: analyses the latest window now
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
see: melbands~ mfccgram descriptors~ knn kmeans

@ chroma~
syntax: chroma~ [size] [hop]
The live pitch classes of its signal: a list of 12 values, C first, scaled to
a maximum of 1 (all 0 in silence), every `hop` samples while audio runs. The
live version of chromagram.
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 256 to 32768 (4096)
arg hop: the step between analyses, in samples, 16 or more (1024)
msg bang: analyses the latest window now
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
see: chromagram keyfind melbands~ plot

@ onset~
syntax: onset~ [threshold] [size] [hop]
A bang when a note or a hit begins in its signal: the spectral flux peaks
above `threshold` times its median over the last 21 frames, at least `gap` ms
after the previous onset, while the level is above `gate` dB. The right
outlet gives the flux every frame. With the default size and hop the bang
comes about 6 ms after the attack enters the window.
arg threshold: how far above the median a peak must be, as a factor (2)
arg size: the window, in samples, a power of 2 from 64 to 32768 (1024)
arg hop: the step between analyses, in samples, 16 or more (256)
msg bang: analyses the latest window now
msg threshold x: sets the threshold factor (2)
msg gap ms: the shortest time between two onsets (50)
msg gate dB: the level under which nothing counts (-50)
msg hop n: sets the step (samples)
see: onsets descriptors~ beats peakamp~
